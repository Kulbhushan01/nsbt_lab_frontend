Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

These terms cover use of the NSBT Library Management System by students, faculty and staff of Nath School of Business & Technology, MGM University. By logging in, you agree to them.

1. Account access

Your login is issued by the library and is for your own use only. Don't share your password or let someone else issue or return books under your account. You're responsible for activity recorded against your login.

2. Borrowing rules

RuleDetail

3. Fines

Fines are calculated automatically when a book is returned late, and are recorded against your account until marked paid by library staff at the desk. Outstanding fines may affect your ability to issue further books, at the library's discretion.

4. Account suspension

The library may suspend an account for repeated late returns, damage to books, or misuse of the system (e.g. sharing login credentials). You'll be notified of the reason and how to resolve it.

5. Accuracy of records

The system reflects the library's records at the time of each transaction. If you believe a due date, fine, or issue record is wrong, raise it with library staff so it can be corrected in the system.

6. Changes to these terms

The library may update these terms from time to time to reflect changes in policy (for example, the fine rate or loan period). The version in effect is the one shown on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms can be directed to the library desk at NSBT, or by email to library@nsbtmgmu.edu.in.
