Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information the NSBT Library Management System collects from students, faculty and library staff, and how it is used. It applies only to this library portal, not to other NSBT or MGM University systems.

1. Information we collect

When the library issues you a login, we store:

  • Your name, email address and role (student, faculty or admin)
  • Your registration number or employee ID and course/department, if provided
  • Your login password, stored as a one-way hash. Library staff cannot view your actual password
  • Your borrowing history: which books you've issued, issue and due dates, and return dates
  • Any fines charged to your account and whether they've been paid

We do not collect payment card details, government ID numbers, or any information beyond what is needed to run the library catalog.

2. How this information is used

  • To let you log in and see your own issued books and fines
  • To let library staff track which copies are checked out and to whom
  • To calculate late fines (currently ₹5 per day past the due date)
  • To generate usage reports for the library (e.g. total books issued, fines collected). These reports show aggregate numbers, not individual borrowing habits, to anyone other than library staff

3. Where this information is stored

All records are stored in the library's own database on the server the college runs this system on. Your session login token is stored in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between page loads; it is cleared when you log out or expires automatically after 12 hours.

4. Who can see your information

Library staff (admin accounts) can see your name, contact details, borrowing history and fines in order to manage the catalog. Other students cannot see your individual records. This system does not share your data with any third party, advertiser, or outside organization.

5. How long we keep it

Your account and borrowing history are kept for as long as you're enrolled or employed at NSBT, plus a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. You can ask the library desk to delete your account once you've cleared any outstanding books and fines.

6. Your choices

You can ask library staff to correct inaccurate details on your account (name, course, registration number) at any time. To close your account, return all issued books, clear any outstanding fines, and request account deletion at the library desk.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to the library desk at NSBT, or by email to library@nsbtmgmu.edu.in.
